Your current limit is a joke.

Apple's Screen Time lets you tap "Ignore Limit" and carry on scrolling. Of course you did. You've done it before. Nobody saw. Nobody cared.

Spare fixes the only thing that was ever broken: nobody is watching.

How it works

1

Pool it.

Pick an app and a group. Say 32 minutes of Instagram across four people. That's 8 each.

2

Get locked.

Burn your 8 minutes and the app shuts. No "remind me in 15." No mercy.

3

Beg.

Ask the group for more. They can hand over some of their own time, or leave you on read. Their call.
