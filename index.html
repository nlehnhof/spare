Scroll Squad
iPhone first

Out of time? Go beg.

Scroll Squad is the screen-time limit your friends control. Hit your cap and you're locked out until someone in your group gives you their minutes. Or tells you no.

Free to start. We'll email you when it's ready. Nothing else.

Your current limit is a joke.

Apple's Screen Time lets you tap "Ignore Limit" and carry on scrolling. You've done it before. Nobody saw. Nobody cared.

Scroll Squad fixes the only thing that was ever broken: nobody is watching.

How it works

Pool it.

Pick the apps and a group. Say 32 minutes a day across four people. That's 8 each.

Get locked.

Burn your 8 minutes and the apps shut. No "remind me in 15." No mercy.

Beg.

Ask the group for more. They can hand over some of their own time, or leave you on read. Their call.

See the squad in action.

Your friends get a ping, a vote, and a reputation board to prove who is generous and who is cold.

Can my friends see what I do on my phone?

No. Your group sees minutes given and requests sent. They never see which apps you use or what you do inside them.

What if my friends say no?

Then you're locked out. That's the entire point.
