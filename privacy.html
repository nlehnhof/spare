Scroll Squad

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Short version: Scroll Squad helps you limit your own app time with friends you choose. We don't read which apps or websites you use, we don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we are

Scroll Squad is an independent iPhone app project run by an individual developer. Scroll Squad is currently in development. You can reach us at n.lehnhof01@gmail.com.

What Scroll Squad does

Scroll Squad lets you set a daily time limit on apps you choose and share that limit with a small group of friends. When you reach your limit, Scroll Squad shows a screen asking if you want to request more time. Your group members can approve or decline your request.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your Sign in with Apple identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address or Apple's private relay address, plus the display name you pick.
  • Group information: the groups you join, the group members, the label your group gives its limited app (for example "Instagram"), and the daily time pool.
  • Requests and approvals: when you ask your group for more time, who responded and how many minutes were granted.
  • Limit status: the fact that you reached your own limit, shared only when you choose to ask your group for more time.
  • Notification token: an identifier Apple provides so we can send you notifications about requests.
  • Waitlist email: if you join our waitlist on this website, the email address you enter, handled by our email provider Kit.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not read or store which apps or websites you open, or how long you use them. Scroll Squad uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, DeviceActivity and ManagedSettings). The apps you select appear to Scroll Squad only as opaque tokens that Apple provides. Scroll Squad cannot read their names.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, messages, location or browsing history.

How we use information

  • To run groups, requests and approvals.
  • To send notifications about requests.
  • To keep the service working and secure.
  • To email waitlist members about Scroll Squad's launch.

We do not use Screen Time data or any other information for advertising, profiling or data brokering, and we do not sell your information.

Who sees your information

Members of a group you join can see your display name and the requests you send them. Other users cannot. We use service providers to run Scroll Squad, such as a hosting and database provider, Apple's notification service and Kit for the waitlist email. They process data only to provide those services.

Your choices

  • You can leave a group or remove your limit at any time.
  • You can revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and associated data by emailing us. You can unsubscribe from waitlist emails with the link in any email.

Retention

We keep account and group data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account and group data, except where we must keep information to meet legal obligations.

Children

Scroll Squad is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the app or by email.

Contact
